Who We Are

A Nigerian Business Group Built for the Long Term

01 / 06

Petrocell Group Limited is an integrated Nigerian holding company with operating subsidiaries across the energy and connectivity sectors. Founded in Port Harcourt, the commercial heart of Nigeria's energy industry, Petrocell Group was established with a clear mandate: to build world-class industrial businesses that create lasting value for Nigeria.

Through strategic investments in oil and gas services, downstream energy, and telecommunications infrastructure, the Group is building a diversified industrial platform that supports Nigeria's energy security, economic growth, and digital connectivity, for this generation and the next.

Chimezie Ifeanyi SamuelFounder & Group Chairman
Petrocell Group Limited

Chimezie Ifeanyi Samuel is the Founder and Group Chairman of Petrocell Group Limited, an integrated Nigerian industrial holding company headquartered in Port Harcourt. A Software Engineering graduate of the Federal University of Technology, Akure, and a specialist in AI systems engineering and multi-agent automation, Chimezie combines deep technical expertise with entrepreneurial vision to build businesses at the intersection of industry, technology, and impact.

Under his leadership, Petrocell Group is developing an integrated industrial platform spanning energy services, connectivity infrastructure, and technology, positioning the Group as one of Nigeria's emerging multi-sector business groups.
